# Renewal and discernment editorial review

The Kingdom course now continues through Nuggets 12–14: guarding what enters the inner life, answering temptation with Scripture and a practical exit, and testing an apparent prompting with patience. Nugget 12 distinguishes the biblical *heart* from the modern psychological term *subconscious*. Proverbs 23:6–8 describes an unwilling host whose words hide his motives; it is not a formula that thoughts create reality. The broader biblical call to renew the mind is better grounded in Romans 12:1–2, Mark 4:24, and Romans 10:17.

Repetition belongs in the practice as attentive hearing and a stable routine, not as a promise to erase memories or guarantee holiness. The goal meditation exercise therefore pairs a recurring trigger with a fitting passage and a concrete response. Users can revisit that passage morning, midday, and evening. A private notebook already holds their own words; selecting a response persists only a passage choice key for that Goal. The prepared prayer **Renew my mind** asks for grace and action instead of claiming an automatic mental reset.

Discernment requires both openness and testing. Acts 10:9–23 says Peter was in doubt about his vision and pondered it until further events clarified its meaning. First Thessalonians 5:19–21 urges believers not to quench the Spirit and to test everything. A confident first impression may still carry pride, fear, or misunderstanding; doubt may be honest reasoning, a safety signal, or fear. The course asks whether an impression agrees with Scripture, grows love, respects safety, and bears good fruit, with wise counsel when others may be affected.

New birth is real, while the renewal of habits and desires continues. The lessons do not teach a rigid mechanism in which an already saved spirit can be damaged by a soul that is merely programmed by media. Body, soul, and spirit are treated as the whole person before God. A passing intrusive thought is not labeled a chosen sin. Persistent compulsive behavior may need trusted and skilled help alongside prayer.

The existing daily video progression already curates BibleProject and Ascension Presents videos. Its alternatives sheet now links to those official sources and Munroe Global's official channel for voluntary further teaching. No unverified third-party reupload enters the curated daily sequence.
