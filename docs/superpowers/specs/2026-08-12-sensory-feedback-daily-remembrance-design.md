# SoulBuddy Sensory Feedback and Daily Remembrance Design

**Date:** 2026-08-12

**Status:** Approved
**Platforms:** Flutter mobile and web, Laravel sync API

## Purpose

Make SoulBuddy feel calm, responsive, and dependable in daily use while strengthening its central work: helping a person pray, act, record, and remember. Sensory feedback must support attention and memory without turning prayer into a game or implying that the app can measure spiritual worth.

This slice improves:

- semantic haptics, restrained sound, and purposeful motion;
- the in-app prayer reminder and reminder-setting experience;
- prayer-session completion feedback;
- Remember and Record navigation;
- a daily private remembrance practice;
- settings, accessibility, offline behavior, and emulator-quality polish.

## Product principles

1. **The app is a humble tool.** It helps the user remember and act; it does not mediate or score the user's relationship with God.
2. **Remembering is substantive.** The app helps preserve deeds of the Lord, personal encounters, Scripture, people, lessons, testimonies, promises, and what the user has chosen to leave behind for God's love.
3. **Original records remain authoritative.** Later reflections are dated additions. The app never rewrites a remembered encounter or claims new divine meaning for it.
4. **Feedback serves cause and effect.** Haptics, sound, and motion confirm an action or attract attention to a due reminder. They are not decoration.
5. **No cosmetic spirituality.** No streaks, scores, rankings, spiritual-capital counters, shame prompts, or routine confetti.
6. **Privacy is visible in the design.** Sensitive content never appears in lock-screen copy, logs, analytics payloads, or background previews.
7. **Offline use is normal.** Recording, recall, settings, prayer confirmation, and reminder fallback work from local data first.

## Sensory language

SoulBuddy exposes semantic feedback intents rather than allowing screens to choose arbitrary vibrations or sounds.

| Intent | Haptic | Sound | Visual response |
|---|---|---|---|
| Selection/navigation | selection click | none | selected state |
| Saved/recorded | light impact | optional soft tap | short check morph |
| Prayer recorded | medium impact | optional gentle chime | gold ripple and check |
| Warning/error | light warning pulse | none by default | inline error and focus |
| Rare factual milestone | medium-heavy impact | optional milestone chime | brief light motes |
| Undo/dismiss | selection click | none | smooth removal |

The rare milestone in this slice is the 40th non-retracted private prayer session for one current prayer revision, which makes the prayer eligible for the later publication flow. The feedback celebrates reaching an available action; it does not describe the prayer as spiritually superior.

### Defaults and preferences

- Haptics are enabled by default where the platform supports them.
- Quiet in-app sounds are enabled by default but can be disabled independently.
- Narration is a separate opt-in capability and never auto-plays.
- The app respects the platform's reduced-motion preference. An explicit SoulBuddy reduced-motion switch may further reduce motion but never overrides a platform request to reduce it.
- Sound playback uses an ambient/sonification audio session so silent-mode and platform audio expectations are respected.
- Web safely degrades to visual feedback; unsupported haptics are a no-op.
- Preferences are local to a device and available before authentication or network access.

### Audio provenance

The Elbiblio audio directory is reference material only. Its manifest contains incomplete source URLs, duplicate placeholder assets, and game-like effects. No such file is copied into SoulBuddy merely because the manifest labels the directory CC0.

SoulBuddy ships only:

- small original tones generated by a committed deterministic asset script; or
- externally sourced audio with a complete source URL, author, license version, retrieval date, and checksum.

The initial set is limited to a short soft tap, a gentle completion chime, and a restrained milestone variation. There is no game-over, level-up, heartbeat, countdown, tick-tock, ambient loop, or transition-whoosh sound.

## Prayer reminder experience

### System notification

The lock-screen notification remains deliberately generic:

- title: `Prayer reminder`;
- body: `Time set aside for prayer`;
- visibility: private;
- no prayer body, encounter excerpt, sensitive title, or generated text;
- system actions: `I prayed this`, `In 10 minutes`, and `Dismiss`;
- ordinary notification sound is controlled by the operating system;
- narration never starts from the notification.

Opening the notification routes to the exact saved prayer and immutable revision represented by the occurrence.

### In-app focus card

When SoulBuddy is open and a reminder becomes due, it shows one compact focus card above navigation:

- prayer icon, title, and scheduled time;
- a quiet ochre focus edge/halo;
- `Open prayer` as the primary action;
- compact `I prayed this` and `Snooze` actions;
- `Dismiss` in an overflow menu;
- no prayer body on the compact card;
- one 180–240 ms fade/slide entrance and one attention haptic;
- no repeating pulse, forced modal, or blocked navigation.

If more than one reminder is due, the first card includes a private count such as `2 prayers are ready`. It does not stack cards or expose titles for every due prayer.

Completing the reminder changes the primary control into a check, emits completion feedback once, removes the due occurrence, and preserves the existing five-minute Undo path. Duplicate taps and repeated notification deliveries remain idempotent for the exact occurrence key.

### Reminder settings

The reminder sheet keeps option-led scheduling and adds:

- clear Once, Every day, and Selected days choices;
- a plain-language schedule summary;
- explicit notification status for this device;
- `Test reminder`, `Pause`, and `Remove reminder` actions where applicable;
- disabled and busy states that cannot be double-submitted;
- timezone-safe rescheduling and daylight-saving handling;
- generic recovery copy that does not expose database or notification details.

## Prayer detail and narration

`I prayed this` remains a factual private record. It receives the approved completion feedback and retains exact Undo. The session count remains quiet and does not become a progress ring.

`Listen` belongs on the opened prayer, not on the compact reminder card unless a cached audio asset is already available. Narration remains governed by the existing approved Deepgram design:

- explicit narration consent;
- immutable revision as source;
- one backend-generated audio asset per content/voice configuration;
- private short-lived playback URL;
- local device cache for later offline playback;
- visible play, pause, seek, and stop controls;
- no automatic playback;
- narration failure never blocks reading or confirming prayer.

## Record and Remember

### Memory record types

The record domain expands from an encounter-only model to typed private memory records. Initial types are:

- Encounter;
- Promise;
- What I left behind;
- Revelation;
- Scripture;
- Person;
- Family;
- Lesson;
- Testimony.

`Promise` stores the user's own commitment or personal covenant wording. `What I left behind` may contain highly sensitive material such as lying, laziness, pride, pornography, or another habit the user names. `Revelation` records what the user believes they received or understood. SoulBuddy stores these labels without certifying that a covenant or revelation came from God.

Records support:

- title, body, occurred date, type, and optional Altar status;
- a `Sensitive` flag enabled by default for `What I left behind`;
- optional Scripture links;
- recall enabled/paused state and recall frequency;
- immutable source revisions;
- dated reflection notes that never overwrite the source record.

Existing encounters migrate to type `Encounter` without data loss.

### Record experience

Record presents only working choices. Every visible type opens a real form; unavailable future features are omitted rather than showing `Coming soon` after a tap.

Forms remain short and option-led:

- choose type;
- add a short title;
- record what happened or what was understood;
- choose date;
- optionally mark Altar, Sensitive, and Include in daily remembrance;
- save locally first.

After save, the user sees a short check response with `View in Remember` and `Record another`. Sensitive text is never included in the completion message.

### Remember experience

Remember becomes an active memory library:

- an empty-state `Record what happened` action;
- type filters and search when useful;
- type icon, date, title, excerpt, and Altar marker on each card;
- sensitive records can hide their excerpt until opened;
- `Return to this` opens the record, its linked Scripture, and dated reflections;
- predictable loading skeletons, empty states, retries, and offline presentation.

Altar is a strong but restrained visual marker, not a tier or score.

## Daily remembrance practice

Today includes one quiet `Remember` card selected from eligible private records. Initial priority groups are:

1. personal promises or covenants recorded by the user;
2. what the user has chosen to leave behind for God's love;
3. recorded revelations and spiritual encounters;
4. Scripture, people, family, lessons, and testimonies as they become available.

Selection is local-first, deterministic for a local date, excludes paused records, avoids choosing the same record repeatedly when alternatives exist, and remains stable for the displayed day. A user can choose another eligible memory without changing the underlying record.

The focused recall view shows:

- the original record and date;
- linked Scripture where present;
- previous dated reflections;
- `Remembered`;
- `Add what I understand now`;
- `Pray with this`;
- `Not today`.

`Add what I understand now` creates a new dated reflection. `Pray with this` starts the existing guided prayer flow with explicit selected context; it does not silently send private content to an AI provider. `Not today` postpones only this recall occurrence.

Each record can be excluded, paused, or assigned a recall interval. The practice has no timer, streak, failure state, score, or celebration. `Remembered` receives only a gentle haptic and quiet visual acknowledgement.

## Today and navigation polish

Today keeps `Pray. Act. Remember.` as its simple structure. The empty state gains two useful actions:

- `Choose a prayer`;
- `Record something to remember`.

When data exists, Today shows at most:

- the selected prayer and practical action;
- the next relevant prayer reminder;
- one daily remembrance card.

It does not become a metric dashboard.

Navigation selection receives selection haptics on supported devices. Five destinations remain available, but compact-width and 200% text behavior must avoid label collisions or truncated actions.

## Settings

A small `Sound & touch` settings surface provides:

- Haptics;
- In-app sounds;
- Reduced motion;
- Narration preferences/status;
- Notification permission and device reminder status;
- a short `Test feedback` control.

Settings are device-local. Turning feedback off prevents future feedback immediately and does not affect reminder persistence or prayer/session records.

## Motion and visual focus

- Ordinary transitions last 150–300 ms.
- No more than two animated elements are active on a screen.
- Due reminders may animate once on entry; they never breathe or pulse indefinitely.
- Normal completion uses a check morph and soft ochre ripple.
- The 40-session eligibility event may show 8–12 slow, low-contrast light motes for less than 900 ms.
- Reduced motion replaces movement and particles with an immediate state/color change.
- Confetti cannons, bouncing controls, flashing glows, and infinite animation are prohibited.

## Data and sync

The implementation uses synchronized typed memory records and reflections, plus device-local preferences and daily presentation state.

Required concepts:

- memory type and sensitivity on the existing encounter lineage or its explicitly migrated successor;
- immutable record revisions containing all user-authored source fields;
- append-only memory reflections;
- recall preferences and last/next recall state;
- local-only sensory preferences;
- local-only daily recall selection/postponement state.

All synchronized writes are owner-scoped and offline-first. Server mutation validation preserves legacy encounter compatibility during migration. Sensitive content is allowed in encrypted transport and the user's private database but excluded from logs, notifications, telemetry, error messages, and provider requests unless the user explicitly selects it under the existing AI disclosure.

## Accessibility and production quality

The slice must verify:

- minimum 48 px primary touch targets;
- 200% text scaling without overflow or hidden actions;
- screen-reader labels and state announcements;
- visible keyboard focus and Enter/Space activation on web;
- Android back behavior for sheets and focused recall;
- light and dark themes with WCAG AA text contrast;
- system and explicit reduced motion;
- sound-off and haptic-off behavior;
- safe areas and compact navigation;
- offline save, process restart, and database reopen;
- reminder permission denied, revoked, and unavailable states;
- no private content in notifications or logs;
- smooth emulator interaction without dropped-frame-prone continuous effects.

## Failure behavior

- Feedback service failure is silent and never blocks the underlying action.
- Missing or corrupt sound assets fall back to haptic/visual feedback.
- Unsupported haptics are ignored.
- Reminder scheduling failure keeps the saved reminder and explains that device notifications are off.
- Daily recall selection failure leaves Today usable and offers a bounded retry.
- Reflection save failure preserves entered text and exposes a generic retry.
- Sync rejection appears in the existing private sync-issue mechanism without leaking content.

## Explicit exclusions

- social publication workflow implementation;
- public profiles or public prayer metrics;
- streaks, points, levels, leaderboards, rankings, and spiritual-capital scores;
- automatic narration or ambient audio;
- AI interpretation of revelations, covenants, or encounters;
- app claims that a record, promise, or insight came from God;
- shame-based reminders or failure language;
- routine confetti or milestone spam.

## Acceptance outcomes

1. A user can create each initial memory type offline and find it in Remember after restart.
2. Existing encounters survive migration as Encounter records.
3. Today offers one stable private recall and the user can remember, reflect, pray with it, postpone it, or exclude it.
4. A due prayer reminder is noticeable, compact, private, non-blocking, and idempotent.
5. Haptic, sound, and reduced-motion settings take effect immediately and persist locally.
6. Ordinary completion is calm; the 40-session eligibility event is rare, factual, and non-gamified.
7. No borrowed placeholder audio or unverified license enters the application bundle.
8. Flutter tests, Laravel tests, Chrome integration, Android emulator integration, accessibility checks, static analysis, and production builds pass.
