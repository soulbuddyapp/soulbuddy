# SoulBuddy First-Use Ritual

## Purpose

The welcome should help a new person understand SoulBuddy in under a minute and immediately begin the same rhythm that makes the app useful each day: pray, act, remember.

SoulBuddy is presented as a quiet aid to personal communion with God, never as a substitute for that relationship or as a measure of spiritual worth.

## Experience

Three brief scenes appear only on first use:

1. **A place to return to God** — SoulBuddy helps the person prepare a personal daily prayer and return to it with attention.
2. **Remember what God has done** — Abraham built altars where God met him. SoulBuddy keeps encounters, promises, Scripture, people, and lessons close.
3. **Carry prayer into life** — Pray, take one faithful step, and keep what is received. SoulBuddy remains a quiet aid as God forms character and makes a life a blessing.

Each scene uses the existing limestone, warm-light, and covenant-gold visual language. The natural slabs gain one illuminated inscription at a time, making the daily rhythm visible without points, streaks, or competitive language.

The final primary action is **Begin with prayer**. It completes the welcome once and opens the prayer library. A small **Go to Today** action remains available throughout so the experience respects the person's agency; it also completes the welcome and opens Today.

## Daily handoff

The existing Today experience continues the sequence:

- choose a prayer;
- pray it and carry its practical action into the day;
- record an encounter, promise, revelation, Scripture, person, family, or lesson for remembrance.

The empty Today guide is revised to name this rhythm gently and keep both useful starting points visible without scrolling.

## Persistence and resilience

Completion is stored locally in secure storage. Until the value is read, a quiet branded loading surface is shown rather than flashing the full app. If storage is unavailable, the app remains usable and treats the welcome as not completed for that launch. A failed completion write does not trap the person: the app proceeds for the current session and can retry on a later launch.

## Accessibility

- Every page has a semantic scene label and readable text.
- Navigation targets meet minimum touch sizes.
- The layout scrolls at large text scales but fits a typical phone viewport at normal scale.
- Page motion follows the system's reduced-motion preference.
- No spiritual score, streak, rank, or implied judgment is introduced.
