# SoulBuddy Guided First Prayer

## Purpose

The first-use experience must carry a new person through one useful SoulBuddy rhythm without expecting them to understand the prayer library, filters, personalization framework, memory categories, or advanced settings.

The journey remains a humble aid to personal communion with God. It uses short, familiar language and presents one easy choice at a time.

## Entry

The three welcome scenes remain. Their final action becomes **Prepare my first prayer** and opens the guided first-prayer journey instead of the prayer library.

A person can choose **Go to Today** at any point. Progress is kept locally so an interrupted journey can resume without repeating completed choices.

## Prayer choices

Each screen presents one question and large answer choices.

### What are you bringing to God?

- Stay close
- Guidance
- Family
- Healing
- Forgiveness
- A difficult time

This determines the prayer purpose. No typed explanation is required.

### How often?

- Daily
- Once

This determines whether the prayer is daily or situational.

### Who is this for?

- Me
- Someone
- My family
- Others

This determines the prayer's reach and beneficiary emphasis.

### Which feels more natural to you?

- Christian
- Catholic
- Jesus
- All of the above

This is a content-ranking preference, not a declaration of denomination or a doctrinal test.

The ranking behavior is:

| Choice | Content preference |
| --- | --- |
| Christian | General Christian first, direct Jesus-centered prayer next, Catholic last |
| Catholic | Catholic first; General Christian and direct Jesus-centered prayer remain available without a required order between them |
| Jesus | Direct, personal, Gospel-centered prayer first, General Christian next, Catholic last |
| All of the above | No ranking preference; General Christian, Catholic, and direct Jesus-centered prayer are all acceptable |

The preference controls the initial draft and later prayer ordering. It must not hide acceptable prayer types unless the person changes their settings to do so.

### Add anything?

- Speak
- Write
- Not now

Speaking or writing adds one optional personal detail. SoulBuddy does not require a title, category, or long explanation. The interface says only **Preparing your prayer…** while creating the draft and does not expose infrastructure or provider names.

## First prayer

The prepared prayer appears alone, without library filters, vote counts, or competing cards. The primary actions are:

- **Listen** when narration is available
- **Pray now**
- **Keep this prayer**

If personalized preparation is unavailable, SoulBuddy quietly offers a suitable prepared prayer based on the person's choices. The person is never stranded on an error screen.

After the person confirms the prayer, SoulBuddy presents **Practice** followed directly by the prayer's practical action. If the prayer has no practical action, this section is omitted.

## First remembrance

After prayer and the practical step, SoulBuddy asks:

**Before you leave, record something you would like to remember.**

- Encounter
- Promise
- Scripture or lesson
- Nothing today

After a remembrance type is selected, SoulBuddy asks:

**Speak or write?**

The first-use recorder contains one main response field or one audio control. SoulBuddy supplies a private title from the remembrance type and date. Advanced fields, sensitivity controls, Altar options, and category management remain available when editing later but do not interrupt first use.

## Completion

The journey ends on Today with the chosen prayer and any completed action or remembrance already visible. Later visits use the normal Today, Prayers, Record, and Remember experiences.

The prayer library remains available as an optional secondary route during the guided journey through **Choose a prepared prayer**, but a new person is never dropped into the full library without context.

## Privacy and resilience

- User-facing spiritual screens describe value and choices, not vendors or technical architecture.
- Provider and subprocesser details belong in the privacy policy and required legal disclosures, not routine spiritual copy.
- Only information explicitly added during the guide can be used to prepare the prayer.
- First-use progress and recordings are local-first.
- Offline or service failure falls back to a suitable prepared prayer.
- A persistence failure must never trap the person in onboarding.

## Accessibility and restraint

- One decision per screen.
- No required open-ended writing.
- Short labels and sentences wherever meaning remains clear.
- Speak and Write are equal paths.
- Large touch targets, screen-reader labels, reduced-motion support, and large-text layouts are required.
- No spiritual scores, streaks, ranks, claims of usefulness, or technical provider language.
