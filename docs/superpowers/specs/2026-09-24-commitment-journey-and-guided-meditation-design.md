# Commitment Journey, Formation, and Guided Meditation Design

**Date:** 2026-09-24
**Status:** Approved revised design
**Platforms:** Flutter Android, iOS, and authenticated web

## Purpose

Give every SoulBuddy user one private, guided opportunity to bring their needs,
calling, healthy practices, and surrender honestly before God, choose a regular
meditation rhythm, and later practice listening and discernment.

The experience is an invitation, not a spiritual test. It must help a person
take something seriously without implying that God’s presence, grace, readiness,
or approval is produced by completing a form. The one-time journey creates a
separate Commitment record, a linked actionable Goal, and a factual preparation
cycle. It does not create a score, streak, level, or public spiritual status.

The design is rooted in the existing SoulBuddy **Covenant and Grace** language:
God is present before any user action, formation is grace, and the app is a
humble aid rather than a source of spiritual authority. The immersive sequence
uses the already approved sanctuary artwork for this specific experience.

This design replaces the current first-Goal experience for fresh users while
preserving resumable legacy onboarding. It offers the journey to existing users
without forcing it on them.

## Product decisions

- A fresh user encounters the Commitment Journey after welcome and Sacred Hours
  setup when no prior Goal onboarding progress exists.
- The user may choose **Not now** at any point. The draft remains local and the
  person may continue later from Goals.
- Existing users who do not have a Commitment see a non-blocking invitation in
  Goals. Existing app launch behavior is otherwise unchanged.
- Commitment identity is one separate synced entity. A user has at most one
  primary Commitment in this release.
- The Commitment links one-to-one to an actionable Goal.
- The user writes exactly three needs from God and one calling, then creates
  between one and twelve healthy daily practices. Practices are flexible in
  number and tagged with one of the user’s requested categories.
- Every completed journey includes an editable, user-reviewed Activity Session
  declaration. Lines are deterministic local template content, not generated by
  an AI provider.
- The journey offers three reviewed prayer-guide types, each with bundled voice
  guidance. It does not add voice narration to the entire prayer library.
- Generic reviewed guidance is the default. Optional narration of a saved
  personal prayer uses the existing explicit-consent, immutable-revision
  Deepgram flow and never includes other Commitment answers or the declaration.
- The scheduled spoken invitation uses a short bundled TTS recording as an
  Android/iOS notification sound. It is not runtime device TTS and does not
  require a long-running background service.
- The full meditation and listening sessions remain foreground-only. Leaving or
  backgrounding pauses the timer and stops audio truthfully.
- Finishing a session records a private factual row only when the user ends it
  explicitly or the timer completes naturally.
- Recording a memory after a session is optional and uses the existing Record
  experience.
- After finishing, the app offers a factual ten-day preparation review and a
  user-led Listening and Discernment stage. It never says the user is ready,
  approved, mature, or filled with the Holy Spirit.

## Formation and theological guardrails

The product may teach conviction, surrender, Scripture, and divine calling, but
it must not shame a person for fear, delay, disability, poverty, mental health,
or an ordinary inability to perform religious action.

- Light represents God’s prior presence and attention, not an achievement
  unlocked by app activity.
- The figure in the artwork represents a person, not a literal anatomical body
  and soul.
- Formation includes spirit, mind, body, desires, habits, work, and relationships.
  It is not an instruction to escape or despise the body.
- Prayer is not dependent on a perfect posture or a completely silent room. The
  app encourages intentional turning toward God when possible without declaring
  other prayer invalid.
- A declaration is an honest response and a memory aid, not a magical formula
  or proof of transformation.
- Moses’ waiting is presented as formation in God’s timing, not as a promise
  that every delayed desire will receive the desired outcome.
- The app does not diagnose the user’s psyche or claim that a practice is
  universally healthy. Suggestions are reviewed options, not prescriptions.

## Information architecture

The new top-level routes are:

| Route | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `/welcome/commitment` | Immersive first-use journey outside the normal shell |
| `/commitments/:commitmentId` | Private, read-only Commitment summary |
| `/welcome/commitment/readiness` | Optional readiness teaching before a Commitment is saved |
| `/commitments/:commitmentId/readiness` | Optional readiness teaching after a Commitment is saved |
| `/commitments/:commitmentId/meditate` | Guided meditation preparation and session |
| `/commitments/:commitmentId/listen` | User-led Listening and Discernment session |

`/welcome/goal` remains available for resumable legacy first-Goal progress. It
is not rewritten into Commitment data. The primary shell remains Today, Goals,
Persons, Remember, and Circles; Commitment does not become a sixth destination.

### Goals entry states

Goals reads the Commitment and journey-progress projections and displays one
contextual entry:

- no Commitment and no draft: **Begin your commitment**;
- draft exists: **Continue your commitment**;
- Commitment exists: **View commitment**.

The completed entry opens the Commitment summary and provides links to its
linked Goal, readiness teaching, meditation controls, and Listening stage when
available.

## Journey experience

### 1. Opening audiovisual invitation

The journey begins in a full-screen quiet state. It does not autoplay sound. The
primary action is **Play the invitation**. **Pause**, **Replay**, and **Skip to
quiet place** become available during playback.

The visual uses:

- `apps/soulbuddy/assets/images/sanctuary/ascent.png` for the initial ascent;
- `apps/soulbuddy/assets/images/sanctuary/sacred_hour_portal.webp` for the final
  portal transition;
- existing ivory, stone, and covenant-gold surfaces;
- deterministic, code-rendered light and particles only; no runtime image or
  video generation.

The approved 45-second sequence is:

| Time | Phase | Visual and copy |
|---:|---|---|
| 0–6s | Threshold | The ascent image is dimmed beneath a deep green-charcoal vignette. A small upper light remains visible. Copy: **Be still. You are not alone.** |
| 6–15s | Receiving | Warm light descends from top center; contrast and saturation rise gradually; fine light particles move upward. Copy: **Receive what God has already given.** |
| 15–24s | Presenting | The vignette recedes and the person changes from near-silhouette to warm light. Three faint inscriptions appear on the foreground stone: **Need · Calling · Practice**. Copy: **Present your life honestly before Him.** |
| 24–34s | Ascending | A slow upward pan and four-percent scale move attention toward the radiant figure and path. Copy: **Offer your calling and your daily choices.** |
| 34–41s | Entering presence | The scene crossfades over 1.2 seconds from the ascent image to the portal, keeping both figures near center. The portal brightens once. Copy: **You do not carry this commitment alone.** |
| 41–45s | Abiding | The image is still and fully warm. A gentle bell may resolve the sequence. Title: **A commitment begins in God’s presence.** Primary action: **I’m ready.** |

The inscriptions are descriptive fields, not progress marks. The artwork never
depicts escape from the body, levitation, anatomy, energy points, or a bodily
transformation achieved by the user.

### Invitation narration

The reviewed narration is:

> What you name before God can shape the way you live. A commitment is not
> pretending to be finished. It is presenting your life honestly and asking the
> Father, through Jesus Christ, to reshape your desires, your calling, and your
> daily choices. You do not carry this alone. Bring it into His presence now.

A full transcript is always visible. The user can skip without losing progress.

### Invitation music

The initial implementation uses a 30-second excerpt from **“Aphelion” by Scott
Buckley**, selected for its movement from contemplative to emotionally expansive.
The exact source interval is `00:01:45`–`00:02:15` of the official MP3.

- official source page: <https://www.scottbuckley.com.au/library/aphelion>;
- official download: <https://www.scottbuckley.com.au/library/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Aphelion.mp3>;
- license: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0);
- required in-app credit: **“Aphelion” by Scott Buckley — released under CC BY
  4.0. www.scottbuckley.com.au**.

The asset manifest records the source URL, license text, exact excerpt interval,
checksum, duration, and audio provenance. The credits page links to the official
source. Replacing the music later requires updating the manifest and credits
but must not change the visual or narration timing contract.

If music cannot load, the sequence continues with narration and a silent visual
timeline. Music is never required for access to the journey.

### 2. Optional readiness teaching

Beside **Play the invitation**, a quiet **Why wait? A word about readiness** link
opens `/welcome/commitment/readiness` while the user is still drafting. It is
optional and never blocks the journey. After a Commitment is saved, the same
link opens `/commitments/:commitmentId/readiness` from the summary.

The reviewed teaching contains three parts and a closing reflection:

1. **A real desire before we are ready**
   - Imagine a three-year-old saying they are ready to get married or have a
     home.
   - A good desire can be real before a person is ready to carry its weight.
   - Wanting something is not automatically foolish; timing and formation
     matter.
2. **Moses and the desert**
   - Moses wanted to free his enslaved brothers.
   - The desert became a place of formation before the mission.
   - God’s timing did not cancel the calling; it formed the person who would
     carry it.
3. **Whole-person formation**
   - We are created by God.
   - In Christ, spirit, mind, body, desires, habits, and daily work can be
     brought back to God.
   - Scripture, prayer, a willing attitude, and healthy activity are formative
     practices, not a spiritual performance.
   - Prayer is not dependent on a perfect posture or a perfectly quiet room.

The closing prompt is:

> What am I asking God to give me, form in me, and help me carry?

The teaching is bundled with transcript, narration, and missing-audio fallback.
It has no completion gate and no “readiness” status.

### 3. Quiet-place preparation

After the invitation, the app displays a quiet preparation page with live text:

> Find somewhere quiet, if you can. Stand or kneel if you are able. Lift your
> heart to the heavens in prayer. Get your paper or notebook and a pen.
> Continue when you are ready.

The page does not require location, microphone, camera, or motion permission.
**Continue when ready** is enabled immediately. **Not now** is always available.

### 4. Three needs from God

The prompt is:

> What are three things you need from God for a closer, more faithful walk
> with Him?

Three separately bounded text fields are shown. Each answer is saved before the
user can continue. The copy avoids promising perfection or sinlessness.

### 5. Calling

The prompt is:

> What is one thing you believe God has called you to do or become in this
> world?

The answer is a bounded multiline field and may be written or dictated using
the existing on-device speech-input capability. Speech input is never sent to a
generative provider.

### 6. Healthy daily practices

The prompt is:

> What healthy daily activities can help you pursue this calling in a way that
> glorifies God?

The user creates between one and twelve practices. Each practice has exactly one
category and may be a reviewed suggestion or user-authored text. The app does not
require one practice from every category.

The user-facing categories retain the requested labels:

#### Joy/Pleasure

- Help someone with a concrete need.
- Spend time on a task you genuinely enjoy.
- Read or learn something for pleasure and growth.
- Share a meal, laugh, or celebrate with someone.
- Make something or complete a small creative project.
- Take a pleasant, phone-free break in nature.

#### Exploration/Adventure

- Walk outside without doomscrolling.
- Exercise, jog, stretch, or go to the gym.
- Try a new route, neighborhood, trail, or park.
- Learn a small new skill or hobby.
- Visit a museum, event, or place out of curiosity.
- Bike, hike, or plan a simple active outing.

#### Attention/Self-Esteem

- Spend undistracted time with a trusted friend or family member.
- Join a class, team, or community where you can belong.
- Ask for honest feedback and receive it without performing.
- Serve in a responsibility where your contribution is known.
- Pray, read Scripture, or sit quietly and speak one true sentence to God.
- Finish one small task and name what was done well.

The descriptions frame Attention/Self-Esteem as healthy attention, belonging, and
receiving gifts, not as a diagnosis or score. Every suggestion can be removed,
and **Write my own** is always available.

For a linked Commitment Goal, all linked practices are included in Today before
optional generic Goal resources. The existing three-item per-Goal cap does not
truncate a Commitment’s practices.

### 7. Editable Activity Session declaration

After the practices, the app creates a deterministic local draft from the
user’s answers:

> I, [name], pledge to pursue [calling].
>
> I pray that God takes and leads me as I embark on this journey to know and do
> [calling].
>
> I commit my heart and mind daily to [practice summary].
>
> I surrender my whole self—spirit, mind, body, desires, and daily life—to
> Christ’s renewal.
>
> I plead the blood of Jesus, richly purchased for me, for forgiveness and
> cleansing.
>
> As I write these intentions, may the Holy Spirit guide me.

The user can edit, remove, or add lines. The screen requires **I have prayed
this** after review, but does not require the declaration to contain any
particular line. It is saved as private `CommitmentDeclaration` text, bounded at
4,000 Unicode code points. It is not automatically sent to TTS, Deepgram, or
another provider.

### 8. Prayer-guide type

The journey offers three reviewed types:

| Type | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Surrender** | Present fears, desires, and choices before God |
| **Direction** | Seek clarity around the user’s calling |
| **Peace** | Quiet distraction and receive God’s presence |

Each type has reviewed copy, synchronized captions, a transcript, and a bundled
voice recording. The guide is selected for the Commitment and remains editable
later.

The user may optionally select an existing saved personal prayer. Its existing
narration can be used only after the existing disclosure and consent flow. If
narration is unavailable, the text remains selectable and the generic guide
continues.

### 9. Meditation schedule

The user chooses:

- **Every day** or **Selected days**;
- one or more weekdays for selected-day schedules;
- a local wall-clock time;
- a duration of 5, 10, 15, 20, or 30 minutes;
- **Voice invitation**, **Silent reminder**, or **Off** for this device.

On Android and iOS, **Voice invitation** is the default device choice. Browser
keeps the existing no-op OS reminder behavior. The schedule page explains:

> At the time you choose, Android or iOS may play a short spoken invitation
> through a system notification. It will not include your private commitment.
> Silent mode, Focus, Do Not Disturb, and device volume may suppress it. You can
> choose a silent reminder or turn reminders off.

The schedule is local-wall-clock scheduling with the selected IANA timezone.
It uses ordinary notification precision rather than an exact alarm or
full-screen intent. The OS may delay delivery.

### 10. Commitment name

The final writing step asks:

> What will you call this commitment?

The name is bounded to 120 Unicode code points, normalized, and stored as the
Commitment identity. It also supplies the linked Goal title.

### 11. Summary and finish

The summary shows:

- commitment name;
- three needs from God;
- calling;
- all categorized healthy practices;
- reviewed declaration;
- selected guide type;
- duration and recurrence;
- device reminder mode;
- optional personal prayer link;
- links to the linked Goal, readiness teaching, Begin now, and (when available)
  the ten-day review.

The primary action is **Finish commitment**. **Go to Today** is also available
after the transaction succeeds. The summary contains no celebration animation,
score, public count, spiritual-performance language, or readiness claim.

## Ten-day preparation and Listening

### Preparation cycle

Finishing creates a factual `CommitmentPreparationCycle` with:

- UTC start timestamp;
- local start date;
- start IANA timezone;
- tenth-day local review date;
- state `active`, `review_available`, `listening_opened`, or `deferred`.

The cycle is not a streak. The UI does not display “perfect days,” a percentage,
a level, or a spiritual score. On/after the review date, Today may show:

> **Your preparation review**
> You began this path ten days ago. Would you like to revisit your practices
> and ask for guidance?

The user can choose:

- **Revisit my commitment**;
- **Practice listening**;
- **Not now**.

The transition is user-led. No reminder or date automatically declares the user
ready, approved, or filled with the Holy Spirit.

### Listening and Discernment

`/commitments/:commitmentId/listen` is a focused, optional stage rather than a
new full course. It contains:

- a short reviewed teaching on listening rather than forcing;
- a Scripture/quiet period;
- prompts about what is present, what distracts, and what faithful response is
  possible;
- 5, 10, or 15 minutes of quiet listening;
- the existing still bell;
- the same foreground lifecycle and optional memory handoff as meditation.

Listening sessions use `sessionKind = listening`. They do not create a streak or
Goal completion event.

## Domain model

The implementation lives in `features/commitments/` and has focused domain,
application, data, provider, and presentation units.

### Wire values and limits

- `journey_slot`: `primary`.
- `guide_id`: `surrender`, `direction`, `peace`.
- `frequency`: `daily`, `selected_days`.
- `session_kind`: `meditation`, `listening`.
- `session_outcome`: `completed`, `ended_early`.
- `practice_category`: `joy_pleasure`, `exploration_adventure`,
  `attention_self_esteem`.
- `practice_source`: `reviewed_suggestion`, `user_authored`.
- `cycle_state`: `active`, `review_available`, `listening_opened`, `deferred`.
- `GoalActionKind.commitmentPractice` wire value: `commitment_practice`.

Unicode code-point limits are:

- Commitment name: 120.
- Each need: 500.
- Calling: 1,500.
- Each practice: 1,000.
- Declaration: 4,000.
- Journal/reflection memory remains governed by the existing Encounter limits.

All validation uses `runes.length`, not UTF-16 `String.length`.

### `Commitment`

Durable fields:

- `id`;
- `ownerId`;
- `journeySlot`;
- `goalId`;
- `name`;
- `keyNeedOne`, `keyNeedTwo`, `keyNeedThree`;
- `calling`;
- `guideId`;
- `createdAt`;
- `updatedAt`.

The unique owner-and-slot constraint prevents a second primary Commitment. The
Commitment has no spiritual completion or readiness state. Its name, needs, and
calling are read-only after finish in this release; the selected guide, linked
practices, meditation plan, declaration, and preparation cycle have their
focused edit controls.

### `CommitmentPractice`

Fields:

- `id`;
- `ownerId`;
- `commitmentId`;
- `goalActionId`;
- `category`;
- `body`;
- `position`;
- `source`;
- `suggestionId` (nullable for user-authored practices);
- `createdAt`;
- `updatedAt`.

There may be one to twelve practices per Commitment. `position` preserves the
user’s order. A suggested practice stores the reviewed suggestion ID in a local
content catalog; its copied body is still user-reviewable and editable. The
linked Goal action is the Today projection and is updated in the same local
transaction as the practice.

### `CommitmentDeclaration`

Fields:

- `id`;
- `ownerId`;
- `commitmentId`;
- `body`;
- `reviewedAt`;
- `createdAt`;
- `updatedAt`.

The declaration is private, bounded, and never automatically narrated. The
user can reopen and edit it later; each explicit edit updates the private body
and reviewed timestamp. The app does not present an edited declaration as a new
spiritual certification.

### `CommitmentMeditationPlan`

One plan belongs to one Commitment. Fields are:

- `id` (technical UUID);
- `commitmentId`;
- `ownerId`;
- `frequency`;
- `weekdaysMask`;
- `localTime` in `HH:mm` form;
- `timezone` as an IANA identifier;
- `durationMinutes`: 5, 10, 15, 20, or 30;
- `enabled`;
- `createdAt` and `updatedAt`.

The plan is synced privately. Reminder enablement is not a synced field.

### `CommitmentPreparationCycle`

One cycle belongs to one Commitment. Fields are:

- `id`;
- `ownerId`;
- `commitmentId`;
- `startedAtUtc`;
- `startedLocalDate`;
- `startTimezone`;
- `reviewLocalDate`;
- `state`;
- `createdAt` and `updatedAt`.

The state is factual navigation state only. It does not represent spiritual
maturity, readiness, approval, or success.

### `CommitmentMeditationSession`

A private factual history row contains:

- `id`;
- `ownerId`;
- `commitmentId`;
- `sessionKind`;
- `startedAt`;
- `endedAt`;
- `localDate`;
- `plannedDurationSeconds`;
- `actualDurationSeconds`;
- `outcome`;
- `deviceId`.

A session is recorded only on natural timer completion or explicit **End**.
Backgrounding, route replacement, or process death does not fabricate a
completed session.

### Device-local state

A local-only `commitment_journey_progress` table has one `current` row containing:

- stage;
- readiness-teaching seen state;
- three needs;
- calling;
- guide;
- personal prayer selection;
- declaration draft and reviewed flag;
- schedule fields;
- commitment name;
- deferred state;
- updated timestamp.

A local-only `commitment_journey_practice_drafts` table stores each draft
practice’s ID, category, body, source, and position. A local-only
`commitment_reminder_devices` table stores only reminder mode, next occurrence,
scheduled timezone, stable notification IDs, and reconciliation timestamps. A
local-only `commitment_finalization_recovery` table stores only the finalized
Commitment ID and timestamp. A local-only personal-prayer narration cache stores
bytes and the exact immutable prayer revision; it contains no other Commitment
text.

## Atomic finalization

The `CommitmentFinalizer` uses the existing PowerSync database transaction
boundary. One local transaction creates:

1. the Commitment;
2. the linked Goal and its initial revision;
3. one `commitment_practice` Goal action for every practice, preserving order;
4. the selected personal-prayer/Goal link when applicable;
5. the reviewed declaration;
6. the meditation plan;
7. the preparation cycle;
8. the local recovery marker;
9. deletion of local journey progress and practice drafts.

The transaction does not request notification permission, call Deepgram, or
write external files. Those actions occur only after the local commit succeeds.

If the transaction fails, all local drafts remain and the user can retry. If
the process stops after commit but before navigation, the next launch observes
the recovery marker and Commitment and routes to the summary rather than
creating a second record.

## Sync and server boundary

The Laravel boundary adds authenticated, owner-scoped support for:

- `commitments`;
- `commitment_practices`;
- `commitment_declarations`;
- `commitment_meditation_plans`;
- `commitment_preparation_cycles`;
- `commitment_meditation_sessions`.

Local-only progress, practice drafts, reminder devices, recovery markers, and
narration caches are excluded from upload.

The server derives ownership from the authenticated user, ignores client owner
identifiers, validates all lengths and enum values, verifies Goal, Commitment,
practice, declaration, plan, cycle, and session references, enforces the unique
primary slot, and supports replay-safe mutation operations. A second primary
Commitment is rejected with a sanitized, non-sensitive error. Deleting an
account cascades its private records.

A local write remains usable if sync is unavailable. A sanitized local issue may
record that a duplicate or rejected operation needs attention, but it must never
contain the name, needs, calling, practices, declaration, prayer text, or notes.

## Reminder architecture

`CommitmentReminderCoordinator` reuses the existing native notification boundary
and timezone reconciliation patterns. It owns:

- permission requests;
- voice, silent, and off device modes;
- stable notification identifiers based on Commitment ID and occurrence;
- schedule creation, replacement, cancellation, and reboot restoration;
- timezone-change reconciliation;
- generic notification response routing to
  `/commitments/:commitmentId/meditate`.

The coordinator does not own visual session state or database transactions.

### Spoken notification asset

The bundled voice asset says:

> This is a time to be with God, if you can. Open SoulBuddy when you are ready.

It is a reviewed TTS recording generated ahead of time. Android uses a
versioned notification channel and raw sound resource. iOS uses a versioned
custom notification sound in the app bundle. A missing or invalid sound falls
back to a silent generic notification. OS silent mode, Focus/DND, and volume
remain authoritative.

Notification title and body contain only generic text. They never include the
Commitment name, needs, calling, practices, declaration, prayer, or a readiness
claim. Tapping opens session preparation but never auto-starts a timer.

Browser continues to use the existing no-op scheduler for OS reminders while
retaining the saved plan and manual **Begin now** action.

## Media and asset boundaries

All reviewed static media is bundled and versioned under
`apps/soulbuddy/assets/audio/commitments/`:

- optional readiness narration and transcript;
- invitation narration and transcript;
- the exact Aphelion excerpt and manifest credit;
- Surrender, Direction, and Peace guide audio/transcripts;
- Listening and Discernment audio/transcript;
- the spoken notification master;
- `catalog_v1.json`;
- `ASSET-MANIFEST.md`.

The initial voice recordings may be generated with local Microsoft Zira Desktop
TTS on Windows for the current content. The manifest records that provenance.
Replacing a recording requires updating the manifest, checksum, transcript, and
review state. No user-entered calling, practice, or declaration text is sent to a
TTS provider by default.

The private personal-prayer narration path remains separate and requires the
existing explicit consent. The declaration is not automatically sent to that
path.

The asset manifest records:

- stable asset ID and semantic use;
- transcript or caption source;
- generation or source provenance;
- license and attribution requirements;
- duration, checksum, and format;
- required/optional status;
- exact Aphelion source interval `00:01:45`–`00:02:15`.

## Dedicated meditation and listening sessions

### Preparation

The route is a full-screen quiet surface with one primary **Begin session**
action. It shows the selected guide, duration, optional personal-prayer control,
and a private reminder that no session begins until the user presses the action.

### Guided quieting

Each generic guide is approximately 60–90 seconds and includes reviewed text
with synchronized captions. Every guide includes:

1. **“Be still, and know that I am God.”** — Psalm 46:10, RSVCE, with existing
   Scripture provenance;
2. permission for distracting thoughts to pass rather than being chased;
3. focus on the divine Father, approached through Jesus;
4. presentation of the Commitment, needs, calling, and healthy practices;
5. **Now listen**.

The guide does not read the user’s private fields. If a selected personal prayer
has authorized cached narration, it may play in this phase. The generic guide
remains available if personal prayer playback is unavailable or declined.

The guide plays once and is not looped.

### Listen and timer

After **Now listen**, the app plays `sacred_still_bell.wav` once, waits a short
deliberate silence, and starts the selected timer automatically. The screen
reduces to remaining time, pause/resume, and **End**. The optional existing
meditation sound picker is available during setup; silence is the default.

Leaving the route, opening another session, or backgrounding the app stops
guide/sound audio and pauses the timer. Returning shows the truthful remaining
time. The app does not run a misleading background meditation service.

### Listening and Discernment phase

The Listening route uses the same lifecycle with `sessionKind = listening`. Its
reviewed sequence invites the user to listen without forcing a sensation or
interpretation. It may present one or more prompts for private reflection, but
it does not claim that a particular feeling proves the Holy Spirit’s presence.

### End and memory

Natural completion plays the still bell and records a `completed` session.
Explicit early end records `endedEarly` without penalty or guilt copy. The
ending screen offers:

- **End**;
- **Record a memory**.

The memory composer opens through the existing Remember/Record flow with the
session local date and the prompt **“What do you want to remember from this
time with God?”** `Encounter` is preselected but remains changeable. A written
or locally recorded audio memory can be saved under existing privacy rules.
No memory is created automatically, and no Goal action is marked complete by a
session.

## Goals and Today integration

The linked Goal is shown in the normal Goals list. The Commitment summary shows
all practices as categorized identity content while linking to the existing
Goal practice controls for edits, completion, skip, and retraction.

The existing Today composer includes every linked `commitment_practice` action
on its scheduled dates before optional generic resources. The composer’s normal
per-Goal cap does not truncate a Commitment’s practice set. A meditation or
listening session does not write Goal action events.

A Goal can be archived or paused through existing controls without deleting the
Commitment, practices, declaration, preparation cycle, or private session
history.

## Accessibility and responsive behavior

- The readiness teaching, invitation, and session guides have synchronized
  transcripts.
- All interactive controls meet a minimum 48dp target.
- Text remains usable at 200% scaling; decorative artwork reduces or yields
  space before controls are compressed.
- Light, particles, and artwork are decorative and excluded from semantic
  focus.
- Reduced motion renders stable threshold and abiding states with no particles,
  camera pan, scale movement, or flashing.
- No flashing, strobe, rapid color changes, camera shake, or high-contrast
  pulsing is permitted.
- Audio controls expose play, pause, replay, skip, and mute semantics.
- Timer announcements occur at useful intervals or on request rather than every
  second.
- Notification lock-screen content is generic and contains no private fields.
- The session controls remain usable in portrait, landscape, compact phone, and
  larger layouts.
- The optional readiness teaching is reachable by keyboard and screen reader
  without being required to proceed.

## Recovery and failure behavior

- Missing music: continue with narration and silent visuals.
- Missing readiness or guide audio: show the complete transcript and continue.
- Missing reviewed declaration: show the deterministic editable template and
  require review.
- Missing notification sound: use a silent generic notification.
- Notification permission denied: preserve Commitment, practices, declaration,
  plan, cycle, and Begin now; show a concise recovery explanation.
- Invalid local progress: preserve the last valid fields, discard only malformed
  values, and explain the recoverable state without private content.
- Reminder scheduling failure: keep the local plan and expose retry through
  Settings or the Commitment summary.
- Background/lifecycle interruption: stop audio and pause the timer truthfully.
- Sync rejection: retain the local record and create only a sanitized local
  issue without private content.
- Memory save failure: retain the reviewable text or local recording and offer
  retry or discard through the existing composer.
- Invalid practice suggestion data: omit that suggestion, keep the rest of the
  catalog usable, and record no private content.
- Stale preparation-cycle date: recalculate the factual review date from stored
  start date/timezone without declaring readiness.

## Non-goals

- Multiple simultaneous Commitments.
- Public testimony, sharing circles, or a public Commitment history.
- Spiritual scores, streaks, levels, badges, readiness labels, or comparative
  rankings.
- Runtime AI-authored spiritual copy.
- Automatic declarations or automatic private-text narration.
- Voice narration for every prayer already in the library.
- Background playback of the full meditation or listening session.
- Automatic Goal completion from meditation, listening, narration, or prayer
  playback.
- Automatic memory creation.
- A complete ten-day formation curriculum or a full discernment course.
- Destructive migration of legacy Goal or first-prayer progress.
- Replacing existing Sacred Hour, prayer, Goal meditation, or Record behavior.

## Acceptance summary

The slice is accepted when a new or existing user can begin, defer, resume, and
finish exactly one private Commitment; optionally explore the readiness teaching;
create flexible categorized healthy practices; review an editable declaration;
see all practices in Today; choose a valid daily or selected-day meditation
plan; receive a generic spoken invitation on supported Android and iOS
devices; complete a quiet guided foreground meditation; optionally save a
memory; receive a non-coercive ten-day review; optionally begin Listening and
Discernment; and recover from interruption without duplicate records,
private-data leakage, forced spiritual claims, readiness certification, or a new
top-level navigation destination.
